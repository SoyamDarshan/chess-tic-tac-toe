Chess Tic Tac Toe

Line up all four of your pieces on a 4×4 board before your opponent does.

How it plays
  • Goal. Get all four of your pieces (pawn, rook, knight, bishop) in one row, column or diagonal.
  • Start row. New pieces can only be spawned on your own back row: the bottom row for White, the top row for Black (marked by a bar in your colour).
  • First 3 turns. Each side spawns a piece from home onto its start row.
  • After that. On your turn, either move a piece by chess rules, or spawn a piece from home onto your start row. Once on the board, a piece can travel anywhere, including the enemy side, and capture.
  • Capturing. A captured piece goes back to its owner's home and needs a turn to spawn again. Your fourth piece also starts at home.
  • Wipe-out. You also win at once if you capture the last enemy piece on the board, leaving them with nothing but their home.
  • Your own start row never wins. Any other row, column or diagonal of your four pieces does, including the enemy's start row.
  • Pawns. Step forward one square, capture one square diagonally forward. White moves up, Black moves down. At the far edge a pawn turns around (the arrow shows its direction).
  • No king. There is no check. Rooks and bishops are blocked by any piece in the way.
  • Draw. The side to move has no legal move, or 120 turns pass.
