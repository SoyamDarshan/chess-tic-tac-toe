Chess Tic Tac Toe

A 4x4 board game. Both sides have a pawn, rook, knight and bishop. Spawn pieces on your back row, then move and capture like chess to line up four in a row, or wipe out every enemy piece on the board.

Play now

Demo

A 22-second replay of one real 17-turn game. White and Black each spawn three pieces, trade captures across the board, and White finishes with a diagonal line of four.

How it works

Spawn pieces only on your own back row. After your first three turns you can move a piece like in chess, capture, or spawn a piece from home. A captured piece goes back to its owner's home and costs a turn to spawn again.

Win with four of your pieces in a line (your own back row doesn't count) or by capturing the last enemy piece on the board. Read the full rules.
